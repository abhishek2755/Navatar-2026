01About

This is NAVATAR.

NAVATAR-26 is how the new batch actually meets the campus — not through orientation slides, but through games, small missions, team pressure and a stage at the end of it.

The School of Computer Science and Application, along with the Mavericks Student Forum (SOSCA), puts together four days of Freshers Day activities built around one idea: everyone gets a moment to stand out.
